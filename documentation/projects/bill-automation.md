### Body content

**The problem**

Billing electricity accurately for a commercial mall with over 250 tenants across multiple sites is a moving target every single month — every unit sits on a different consumption tier, every bill has to reach the right tenant through the right channel, and every incoming payment has to be tracked down and matched to the right account before anyone can say who's paid and who hasn't. On top of that, ownership needs clean, accurate recovery and profitability numbers every month. Done by hand, that's a process with a lot of surface area for something to slip.

**Approach**

We designed and built a dedicated billing and recovery platform that owns the entire cycle. Meter readings go in once a month, and the system takes it from there — calculating every tenant's tiered bill automatically, generating a professional PDF invoice, and delivering it by email and WhatsApp, with automatic reminders and escalation for anything that goes unpaid. On the collections side, incoming bank transactions are read directly from daily statements and matched to the right tenant and bill automatically, so payments are applied and reconciled without anyone manually cross-referencing a bank feed. The whole platform runs on a modern, resilient architecture purpose-built to handle the full tenant base every month, reliably, without manual babysitting.

**Outcome**

The mall now runs its entire monthly billing and collections cycle on autopilot, end to end, across every site it operates. Tenants receive accurate, on-time bills through the channels they actually check, overdue accounts are caught and escalated automatically, and the business gets clean, ready-to-use recovery and profitability reporting every month instead of assembling it by hand. A process that used to demand constant manual attention now simply runs itself.
