## 7. Infrastructure Prerequisites — the real blockers

These aren't platform-specific data fields; they're dependencies that block multiple platforms simultaneously if not resolved first. **This is the most important section of this document** for sequencing purposes.

- [x] **Company domain name** — blocks LinkedIn Page creation (requires company-domain email), referenced as a "must-have" prerequisite across nearly every platform document
- [x] **Business email on that domain** (name@boltertechnologies.com) — same blocker as above
- [ ] **Live company website** — explicitly required before listing on GoodFirms ("your business needs to be legally registered and have an official website" — direct quote from their eligibility documentation) and functionally required before DesignRush will accept a submission (they review your existing site, portfolio, and reputation before accepting)

**This means the domain + business email must be resolved before LinkedIn, GoodFirms, or DesignRush — and realistically before most of the other platforms too, since a website link strengthens every profile.** The website is not just "one more platform to set up" — it's a dependency for several of the others.

## What This Reveals About Website Requirements (bridge to the next document)

Now that everything is consolidated, a few gaps become visible that the platform-by-platform research didn't surface, since no single freelance/directory platform needed them — but the website will:

- **Brand guidelines/color palette** (Section 5) — no platform asked for this, but the website needs one
- **Legal pages** — privacy policy, terms of service — not required by any directory, but standard for any company website and likely expected by GoodFirms/Clutch reviewers checking site legitimacy
- **A written "About/Team" narrative** longer than any bio required elsewhere — the website is the only place with no character limit
- **Contact mechanism** (form, not just an email address) — every platform above just needed an email/phone; the website needs a proper intake path since it'll be the place prospects convert
- **SEO/metadata** — none of the platforms above required this, but it's what makes the website findable at all, which is arguably the whole point of it being the "central hub"

These will be the starting point for the dedicated website requirements document.
