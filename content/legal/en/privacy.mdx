Bolter Technologies Private Limited ("Bolter Technologies", "we", "us")
operates boltertech.com. This policy explains what personal data the site
collects, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

We have written this to the standard of the EU General Data Protection
Regulation. Pakistan's own data protection legislation remains in draft at
the time of writing, and we would rather meet the stricter standard our
clients are actually subject to than the local minimum.

## What we collect

**Information you send us.** When you submit the contact form we receive
your name, email address, company name if you provide one, the type of
work you select, and the message you write. We collect this because you
have asked us to respond to you — that is the lawful basis, and it is the
only reason we hold it.

**Analytics.** We use Vercel Analytics, which is cookieless. It records
page views, referrer, approximate country, and device type. It does not
set cookies, does not use browser fingerprinting, and does not build a
profile of you across sites or sessions. Because no personal data is
processed and no cookies are stored, this site does not show a consent
banner — there is nothing to consent to.

**Server logs.** Our host records standard request logs, including IP
addresses, for security and abuse prevention. These are retained by the
host on their own schedule and are not used for marketing.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do
not run advertising or social media tracking pixels on this site. We do
not use your contact form submission to add you to a mailing list.

## Who processes data on our behalf

| Processor        | Purpose                                          | Location                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Vercel Inc.      | Website hosting, cookieless analytics            | United States / global edge |
| Resend           | Delivering contact form submissions to our inbox | United States               |
| Google Workspace | Our business email                               | United States / EU          |

Each of these is a data processor acting on our instructions. Transfers
outside the EEA rely on the processor's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep things

Contact form submissions are kept in our email for as long as the enquiry
is live and for **24 months** afterwards, so that we can pick up a
conversation that resumes later. After that they are deleted. Analytics
data is aggregate and is retained by Vercel according to their own policy.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to access the personal data
we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or
object to how we use it, and to receive a copy in a portable format. These
rights are not conditional on where we are based.

To exercise any of them, email <a href="mailto:contact@boltertech.com">contact@boltertech.com</a>.
We will respond within 30 days. You do not need to explain why you are
asking, and asking will not affect any work we are doing for you.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly you may complain to
your local supervisory authority.

## Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect
personal data from anyone under 16.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of
this page. Substantive changes will not be applied retroactively to data
already collected.
