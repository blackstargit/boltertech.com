## The problem

High-volume digital publishing networks and regional broadcasting outlets
distribute hundreds of news stories daily across a sprawling network of digital
channels, including X (Twitter), Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Threads,
WhatsApp Channels, and web portals. Previously, editorial desks and marketing
leads relied on fragmented, platform-specific portals (such as Meta Business
Suite, YouTube Studio, and Google Analytics), expending dozens of manual hours
each week downloading CSVs and assembling spreadsheets. Without a centralized
data layer, newsroom leadership lacked real-time visibility into cross-platform
story performance, struggled to compare bilingual audience reach across English
and Urdu media, and could not reliably identify breaking content primed for
immediate cross-network amplification.

## Approach

We engineered an end-to-end media analytics platform combining a reactive React
18 Single Page Application with an enterprise Node.js/Express backend and an
optimized Microsoft SQL Server database layer.

To eliminate query bottlenecks across multi-million engagement datasets,
analytical computations were offloaded directly to custom MSSQL stored
procedures with dedicated Node.js connection pooling. The platform introduces a
unified overview console alongside granular channel deep-dives, normalizing
disparate platform metrics into standardized reach, reaction, impression, and
engagement rates. We developed a flexible temporal filtering engine that enables
instant parameter slicing across 24-hour breaking news cycles, 30-day
performance, 6-month trends, 1-year overviews, and custom ISO date ranges.
Additionally, the interface incorporates dynamic state-driven localization to
support full bilingual workflows (English and Urdu) for regional editorial
desks.

## Outcome

Editorial and marketing teams eliminated manual spreadsheet consolidation,
collapsing a recurring multi-hour reporting chore into a live view they can open
on demand. The unified eight-channel dashboard gives newsroom directors instant
visibility into audience engagement dynamics, enabling immediate editorial
pivots, content amplification, and clear cross-network performance benchmarking.
